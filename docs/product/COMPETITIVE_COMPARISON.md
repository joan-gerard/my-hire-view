# MyHireView — Competitive comparison

Living list of where MyHireView **wins** vs competitors, and where it **lags** (things competitors do that we do not, or do worse).

**Default comparison:** Unless an item says otherwise, every entry is vs **Reslink** (the primary competitor). Other vendors get their own section only when we add them.

**Impact:** How much the gap matters when choosing or sticking with a product. Rate **High / Medium / Low**, and always add a short **Impact evaluation** that justifies the rating (who cares, when it shows up, why it is or isn’t a deal-breaker).

| Rating | Meaning |
| ------ | ------- |
| **High** | Likely to change whether someone chooses MHV (or switches from Reslink). Strong pitch / serious gap. |
| **Medium** | Noticeable in day-to-day use; worth mentioning, not a deal-breaker alone. |
| **Low** | Nice-to-have or niche; small edge or small miss. |

**How to add items:** Keep each row short. **Notes** = what one product does and what the other lacks. **Impact evaluation** = why that rating. Prefer shipped product behavior over roadmap ideas. Gaps here are observations, not backlog tickets — promote to [Backlog.md](Backlog.md) when you decide to act.

## Reslink

### Where MHV wins

| Advantage | Impact | Impact evaluation | Notes |
| --------- | ------ | ----------------- | ----- |
| **Edit an application** | High | Job seekers constantly fix typos, swap CVs, update video, or tweak role copy after sharing. Without edit, they must recreate (and risk a new URL / lost analytics). This is day-to-day pain that can drive churn or block adoption once someone hits a mistake — strong switch reason and a clear pitch vs Reslink. | MHV lets candidates edit any application from the dashboard (3-dot menu → **Edit**). No equivalent edit flow found in Reslink. |
| **Getting started checklist** | Medium | Speeds first-session activation toward a shareable link, but candidates rarely pick a tool *because* of a checklist — they notice it after signup. Strong for retention of confused new users; weak as a headline differentiator. | MHV shows a floating **Getting started** panel on `/admin` that guides new accounts through profile → location/link → photo → primary CV → first application → publish/share. Reslink does not show an equivalent checklist. |
| **Quarterly billing (vs annual)** | Medium | Job searches usually last weeks–months, so a 3-month plan matches usage better than a year lock-in and lowers commitment anxiety at checkout. Reslink already offers monthly, so this is not unique “flexibility” — the edge is a discounted middle tier without annual prepay. Helps conversion and messaging; rarely the sole reason to switch. | MHV plans **monthly + quarterly** (no annual) — working draft / `E4-116`. Reslink offers monthly and annual (no quarterly). |

### Where Reslink wins

| Gap | Impact | Impact evaluation | Notes |
| --- | ------ | ----------------- | ----- |
| **Cover photo on applications** | Low | Visual polish on the public page; optional and easy to ignore. Few candidates will leave MHV solely for a banner image when profile picture, CV, and video already carry identity. | Reslink lets candidates add a cover photo that appears on applications. MHV has no cover-photo option — only profile picture (and application media such as CV / video). |
